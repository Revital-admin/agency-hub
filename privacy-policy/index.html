Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy covers the Agency Hub application's connection to QuickBooks Online, used internally by Revital Productions to manage our own company's accounting data. It does not cover Revital Productions' client-facing services generally.

What this integration is

Agency Hub is an internal business-operations tool built and used exclusively by Revital Productions employees and authorized contractors. Its Financial Center feature connects to our own QuickBooks Online company file via the QuickBooks Online API to display and reconcile our own financial figures (cash on hand, revenue, expenses, and related accounts) in one place alongside our other internal tools.

What data we access

Once an authorized Revital Productions team member connects QuickBooks Online, Agency Hub reads:

  • Bank and credit card account balances
  • Profit & Loss (revenue and expense) figures
  • Selected Equity account balances
  • Basic company information (company name)

Agency Hub does not access customer lists, payroll data, or any QuickBooks data belonging to a company other than Revital Productions' own.

How we store and protect this data

OAuth tokens issued by Intuit are stored in our private Google Firestore database, accessible only to Revital Productions' own backend infrastructure. Financial figures pulled from QuickBooks are stored in the same private database and are only ever displayed inside Agency Hub, which is itself restricted to authenticated Revital Productions team members and access-controlled per employee role.

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any third party. We do not use it for advertising or marketing purposes.

Who can access this data

Only Revital Productions team members signed into Agency Hub, and specifically granted the "Finance" role or full admin access, can view this data inside the app.

Disconnecting

An authorized team member can disconnect Agency Hub from QuickBooks Online at any time from within Intuit's own connected-apps management screen, or by contacting us using the information below. Disconnecting stops all further data access; previously synced figures remain in our internal database until manually cleared.

Contact

Questions about this policy or this integration can be sent to admin@revitalproductions.com.
