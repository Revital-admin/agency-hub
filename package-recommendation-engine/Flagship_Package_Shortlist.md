# Flagship Package Shortlist

Pulled from the live Service Pricing Admin catalog (`proposal-calculator/js/service-catalog.js`) — all prices below are the catalog's **default** suggested prices, not confirmed overrides. I don't have direct access to whatever's actually been adjusted/confirmed in Firestore, so treat the numbers as a starting point to sanity-check against real overrides in Service Pricing Admin before pitching.

## Important finding first

The service catalog has **no dedicated video/production line item** — despite that being the actual core differentiator ("AV production company that specializes in marketing"). The only video-adjacent entries anywhere in ~90 services are "Reels & Short-Form Video" ($400/mo, filed under Organic Social) and "Ad Creative Production" ($300/mo). There's nothing for a brand video, a commercial shoot, a live-event AV package, or a standalone production day.

That's worth fixing before finalizing flagship packages — right now the catalog can't actually quote the thing Revital is most differentiated at. Suggest adding a "🎥 Video & Production" category to the catalog with a few real line items (e.g. Half-Day Shoot, Full-Day Shoot, Brand Video Package, Live Event AV Package) before pitching packages built around production work. Happy to add that category to the catalog file if you give me rough day-rate/package numbers to use.

## Shortlist (from what IS already priced)

**1. Brand Foundations Package** — *setup fee, ships immediately*
Brand Strategy Session ($300) + Primary Logo Design ($600) + Color Palette & Typography System ($200) + Brand Style Guide ($400) = **$1,500 setup**
Good first-engagement offer — fully priced already, no new catalog work needed, and a natural on-ramp into a bigger retainer once trust is established.

**2. Social Growth Retainer** — *monthly*
Feed Posts ($350) + Reels & Short-Form Video ($400) + Stories ($150) + Community Management ($250) + Content Calendar Management ($200) = **$1,350/mo**
The clearest recurring-revenue flagship — every line is already priced, and it's the easiest "yes" for a brand that just wants a steady social presence without hiring in-house.

**3. Marketing Audit & Roadmap** — *mixed setup/monthly, low-commitment first step*
Marketing Audit ($600 setup) + Brand Positioning ($400/mo) + Marketing Roadmap Development ($500 setup) = **$1,500 setup + $400/mo**
This is the foot-in-the-door offer — cheaper and lower-risk than committing to a full retainer, and it naturally produces the deliverable that justifies the bigger pitch afterward.

**4. Local Visibility Package** — *monthly*
Local SEO / Google Business Profile ($450/mo) + Google Search Console Management ($150/mo) + Rank Tracking & Reporting ($150/mo) = **$750/mo**
This is the exact service the Google Business Profile checklist just built doubles as delivery methodology for — worth pitching once Revital's own listing is optimized and can be pointed to as proof it works.

**5. Paid Social Launch** — *setup + monthly*
Campaign Setup & Management ($500 setup) + Meta Ads ($600/mo) + Ad Creative Production ($300/mo) + Budget Management & Reporting ($150/mo) = **$500 setup + $1,050/mo**
A heavier commitment — good as a 5th option once the first four are converting, not a starting pitch.

## Recommendation

Lead with #1 (Brand Foundations) and #3 (Marketing Audit & Roadmap) as the two lowest-friction first engagements, with #2 (Social Growth Retainer) as the natural next step once trust is built. Hold #4 until the Google Business Profile checklist work is actually done and provable, and treat #5 as a later-stage offer once there's more delivery capacity confirmed in Team Roster.

None of this replaces finishing the pricing pass across the rest of the catalog per the Business Phase Roadmap's own Phase 1 step 5 — this is just the shortlist of what's ready to pitch first.
