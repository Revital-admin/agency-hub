# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 23, 2026**

Revital Productions ("Revital," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through revitalproductions.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

**Information you give us directly.** When you fill out our contact form or book a discovery call, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, company name, and a description of your project. When you email or call us directly, we retain that correspondence.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we automatically collect certain technical information when you visit the Site, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and how you interact with the Site. We collect this through cookies and similar tracking technologies (see below).

## Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the Site running properly and to understand how visitors use it. This may include analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) that help us see which pages are popular and how visitors navigate the Site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may not work as intended if you do.

We do not currently run third-party advertising on this Site.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Respond to inquiries and discovery call requests
- Provide quotes, proposals, and project communication
- Improve the Site and understand how it's used
- Maintain records of our business relationships
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## Third-Party Service Providers

We work with third-party vendors who help us operate the Site and our business, such as our website hosting/builder platform (Framer), scheduling software for booking discovery calls, email providers, and analytics services. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing purposes.

## Data Retention

We retain the information you provide for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, service any resulting project, and comply with our legal and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to certain uses of it. To make a request, contact us using the information below and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

## Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective Date" above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
